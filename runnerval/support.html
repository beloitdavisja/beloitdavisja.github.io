Common questions

I don't hear the voice coach.
Check that your phone is not on silent, the volume is up, and a voice is available in Settings in the app. Cues use your device's built-in text-to-speech voices.
My run isn't tracking distance or a route.
Allow location access for Runnerval in your device settings, and make sure GPS is turned on in the app. Indoors or on a treadmill, turn GPS off and use the timer only.
Cues stop when the screen locks.
Make sure battery saver or background restrictions are not limiting Runnerval, and that location is allowed while the app is in use.
How do I delete my data?
Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Workouts saved to Apple Health or Health Connect are deleted in those apps.
