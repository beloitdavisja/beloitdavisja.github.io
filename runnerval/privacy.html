Runnerval Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Runnerval is a run-walk interval coach for iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your workouts and runs stay on your device, and we do not collect them.

At a glance

  • No account, no sign-up, no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store or receive your personal information. We run no servers for the app.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • Your location, workouts and routes are stored only on your device, and in Apple Health or Health Connect if you turn that on.

Location

Runnerval asks for location access so it can track an active run. It may keep tracking while your phone is locked or the app is in the background, but only while a run is in progress. Location is not used for advertising or analytics, and it is not shared with us or with third parties by the app. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings; the timer still works without it.

Health data

Saving runs to Apple Health or Health Connect is off until you turn it on. When it is on, Runnerval writes the workout (and, where supported, its distance, route and heart rate) to the health store on your device. On Apple devices the app can also read heart rate from your Apple Watch during a run to display it. Health data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not sent to us or to any third party by the app. You can review or revoke access at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect.

Apple Watch and Wear OS

If you use the watch app, run status and controls are exchanged between your phone and your paired watch using the system connection that Apple and Google provide for that purpose. This stays between your own devices.

Maps

Run routes are shown on a map. On iPhone this uses Apple Maps. On Android it uses Google Maps. To show map tiles, the map provider receives the request your device makes (for example the area being displayed and your device and network details) and handles it under its own privacy policy: Apple, Google. Runnerval does not receive this information.

Notifications and Live Activities

The app can show notifications for the current workout, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and optional gentle reminders after a few days away. These are created on your device. Reminders are off until you turn them on. The app does not use remote push notifications.

Voice coaching

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech voices built into your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Sharing

If you choose to share a run or achievement picture, it is handed to the app you pick in your device's share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app. Runnerval shares nothing unless you do this yourself.

Third parties, advertising and tracking

Runnerval contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites. We do not sell or share your personal information.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. Deleting the app removes the workouts, runs, routes and settings it stored. Workouts saved to Apple Health or Health Connect belong to those apps; delete them there. Because we do not hold a copy of your data, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

Children

Runnerval is a general fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health disclaimer

Runnerval is a training aid and not a medical device. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be described in the app's release notes.
